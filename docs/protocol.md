# Controlled experiments and accounting

Freeze raw-file versions, feature allowlists, label vocabulary, split seed and indices, encoded space, normalization axis and model-shape repair policy before search. Never inspect test metrics to tune candidates, budgets, seeds or search policies. Use search seed42/43/44 and independently restarted final seed10042/10043/10044. Final training uses the same train-only partition, 200 epochs, optimizer and regularization conventions across methods. The held-out test is evaluated at that fixed endpoint. Final results are descriptive until repeated across independent searches.

## Methods

- `bpso`: maximizing BPSO with TableIII settings; 30-epoch evaluations. Corrected global-best inequality and explicit representation choices are in paper_specification.md.
- `bpso_reduced`: identical update equations with fixed min_epochs (3 by default).
- `bpso_sh`: each generation runs successive halving at min_epochs, min_epochs*eta, epochs (default3,9,30). Only final-rung candidates can update personal/global best. Eliminated particles get -infinity for that generation; they retain their previous best. This is a controlled BPSO/SH hybrid baseline, not an asserted literature-exact method or new contribution. Fitness estimates are comparable within best-particle updates.
- `random`: independent uniform binary samples at the maximum fidelity. Fixed inactive bits create phenotype duplicates; the same phenotype cache policy applies to all methods.
- `hyperband`: standard Hyperband brackets and successive halving (Li et al.). Brackets derive from the configured min/max ratio and eta; integer epoch budgets are rounded and reported. All promotion stages resume the same training trajectory.
- `dehb`: upstream DEHB0.1.1, serial worker, continuous [0,1] binary-threshold representation. Differential evolution operates on continuous genes, whereas BPSO operates on bits; the phenotype space is shared but search mechanics are not equivalent. The adapter uses the upstream fidelity API. See [official documentation](https://automl.github.io/DEHB/latest/getting_started/single_worker/) and [upstream repository](https://github.com/automl/DEHB).
- `surrogate`: optional random-forest acquisition over binary encodings, warmup10, 64 proposal pool, 20% random exploration, full-fidelity observations. This is a conventional surrogate search control; no PSO-specific prior-art equivalence or novelty is claimed.

## Budgets and resumption

Two distinct comparison modes: equal-resource (`compute.max_epochs`) and equal-time (`compute.max_seconds`). Epoch count measures actual completed training passes, not equivalent FLOPs: different widths/batches have different costs. Under equal-resource runs, report both actual epochs and wall/GPU seconds. Under equal-time, report actual time and possible one-epoch overshoot. Before a new evaluation the resource manager reserves/checks the complete incremental requested fidelity, so it may leave unused budget at the end. A rejected oversized request stops that method; it is not replaced with a smaller request. State comparison limits and utilization in tables. Hard ceilings on candidates/generations/brackets must be high enough to reach time/resource caps; default paper BPSO intentionally stops after its reported generation count even if caching leaves resources. A fully budget-saturating BPSO variant would depart from paper settings and must be labelled separately.

Search wall time is end-to-end within the search driver (scheduler, cache access, I/O, model construction, training and validation); final model training/testing and preprocessing are separate. Evaluation ledger has per-call wall, training and validation time. GPU-seconds denote synchronized elapsed training+validation occupancy on one GPU; this is not kernel-active time, energy or device-power measurement. On CPU GPU-seconds are zero. Inference time is a separately warmed, synchronized batched test pass; hardware and batch-size dependence remain. No IoT energy savings are claimed.

Every candidate cache includes phenotype, source/split fingerprint, training configuration and training seed. The cumulative epoch is the trajectory lookup key. Identical seed/budget repeated evaluations are deterministic memoization, not independent replicates; varying training seeds yields independent trajectories. One method does not reuse another method's cache in comparisons. Time is charged only for work actually executed, including cache and scheduler overhead in total wall. Promotions charge incremental epochs. Numerical failures are recorded and given -infinity; diagnostic cohorts fail loudly if their paired comparison becomes incomplete. Unexpected exceptions propagate after accounting, rather than silently reporting a valid fitness.

Candidate checkpoints are atomic per completed epoch and include model, optimizer, RNG, data identity and full trajectories. A method scheduler resumes via deterministic replay of its candidate-generation decisions, with cached values from completed evaluations; BPSO also exposes direct swarm state serialization. This can incur replay overhead. Upstream DEHB's own output/resume files are managed by DEHB; our cached evaluator remains scoped by the full experiment config. Time-budget resumption between invocations charges prior persisted wall time, not time while the job was stopped. A hard process kill between checkpoint and ledger persistence can leave a small accounting gap; graceful interruptions are preferable. Concurrent processes must never share a run directory.

## Hypothesis test

Use 25 diverse unique phenotypes (greedy max-min canonical Hamming distance), identical cohort across seeds, all trained at3/10/30. Save validation probabilities, true labels, macro-F1, class recalls and cumulative times. Compare3→30 and10→30 with Spearman, Kendall, top-k overlap, selection regret (`max late F1 - late F1 of early winner`) and premature elimination (`fraction of late top-k outside early retained floor(n/3)`). Stable candidate-index tie-breaking is deterministic; tie counts and undefined constant-rank correlations are reported. No surrogate prediction is used as truth.

Continue top-five at30 plus uniform audit of five early-rejected candidates, plus median/worst at30, to200 using the same weights. These 30→200 statistics describe only the observed subset. To estimate unbiased full-cohort elimination/regret at200, train every candidate to200 (set shortlist to cohort size) or design a probability-weighted audit in advance. Plot per-class recall and quantify whether rare classes' changes coincide with rank reversals. Stratify by training class support, not test performance.

Across search seeds, report individual outcomes and t-based95% confidence intervals; n=1 has no CI. These are independent search runs, not minibatches/epochs treated as replicates. Compare methods on matched partition+seed pairs, inspect failures and compute utilization. The framework saves descriptive summary tables; confirmatory paired tests, multiplicity corrections and power analysis must be prespecified for publication. No claim of significance follows automatically from a plotted curve.

## Ablations and future interfaces

Use separate resolved configs/directories to change one mechanism: budget3 vs10 vs30, Gas column vs row normalization, class weighting, shape-repair versus restrictive search space, promotion fraction, best-update reliability. Protocols in optimization/interfaces.py provide allocation, promotion, trajectory prediction and comparable-budget best acceptance. They are extension seams, not implemented adaptive policies or novelty claims. Any future estimator must train from validation/training observations only and be evaluated against the same budgets and standard baselines.

Reporting separates protocol fingerprints and rejects duplicate random seeds within a method/data/protocol group. Final held-out accuracy-versus-search-time plots are descriptive endpoints, never search feedback. Standalone trajectory figures default to a cap of24 to avoid exporting thousands of candidate plots; every trajectory remains available in JSON and all cost rows remain in CSV. Call generate_reports with a larger max_trajectory_figures for a selected cohort.

Optional `training.class_weighting` is an explicit ablation: `none` preserves the original default, `balanced` uses inverse training class counts, and `sqrt_balanced` uses their square root. Validation/test labels never fit these weights. Weights are normalized to unit mean; weighted cross entropy retains PyTorch's weighted-mean reduction. Keep the setting identical across methods within a comparison. Majority baselines are chosen from training labels only. Collapse/zero-recall diagnostics do not alter fitness or best-particle updates.

Ranking reports now return null for top-k overlap or premature elimination when a tie straddles the relevant selection boundary. Tied early winners have an identifiable regret value only when their late scores agree, otherwise a regret interval is reported. Index-tie-break statistics remain in a separately labelled descriptive field; they are not evidence of stable rankings. Undefined correlations for constant scores remain null.
